Staff Assistant - MEM-273-26
The Office of Congresswoman Maggie Goodlander (NH-02) seeks a dynamic Staff
Assistant to join the team in the district.
To apply, please submit your application via the application portal: Application
The Staff Assistant provides administrative, logistical, casework, and outreach support in
the district office. This position manages front office operations, helps compile and
maintain the Congresswoman’s daily briefing book when in the district, supports interns,
and assists staff in drafting memos and materials. The role also includes driving the
Congresswoman to and from events as needed. The Staff Assistant ensures smooth dayto-day operations of the office and provides essential support for constituent services and
events.
Core responsibilities include answering phones, greeting visitors, and managing incoming
mail; logging and routing casework; compiling briefing binders, event materials, and
memos for the Congresswoman; managing and supervising interns; maintaining office
supplies, organization, and equipment; and performing other duties as assigned.
The ideal candidate will have excellent organizational and multitasking skills; strong
written and verbal communication; and a strong attention to detail and ability to manage
deadlines.
This position is based in Concord, NH with occasional district travel and may require
evening/weekend support. A valid driver's license is required. Salary range is $45,000$50,000.

Scheduler - MEM-268-26
Scheduler – Congresswoman Jennifer McClellan (VA-04)
Location: Washington, D.C.
The Office of Congresswoman Jennifer McClellan (VA-04) seeks a highly organized, detailoriented candidate to serve as her scheduler in her Washington, D.C. office. This position
reports directly to the Chief of Staff and will work closely with both the D.C. and District
staff.
The scheduler will assist in developing, coordinating, and managing all aspects of the
Member’s schedule and logistics in D.C. and in the district. Other responsibilities include
tracking, evaluating, and responding to all invitations and scheduling requests,
coordinating travel for the Member, compiling her briefing materials, and providing
operational support for the office.
Duties include but are not limited to facilitating Member to Member and stakeholder
outreach, coordinating internal and external events, coordinating with the legislative,
communications, and district teams for necessary briefing materials, overseeing
coordination with other Member offices, committee staff, leadership offices, the White
House, executive departments and other federal agencies, coordinating with campaign
and non-official staff on non-official scheduling requests, and other tasks as assigned.
Previous scheduling or administrative experience is required. The ideal candidate will have
excellent attention to detail, strong communication skills, thrive in a fast-paced work
environment, and have previous Hill experience.

This position requires the ability to multitask, work well under pressure, manage
conflicting requests, communicate scheduling changes to the Member, and the flexibility
to respond to urgent scheduling needs outside of normal hours when necessary.
The salary range for this position is $70,000 - $80,000 and includes federal benefits such
as health insurance, retirement plans, generous PTO, paid FMLA, student loan repayment
assistance, transit or parking benefits, and flexible work-from-home options. To apply,
please email a resume and short cover letter or statement of interest
to VA04.Jobs@mail.house.gov.
This full-time position is based in her Washington, D.C. office. Congresswoman
McClellan’s office is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis
of race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age,
disability, or veteran status.
