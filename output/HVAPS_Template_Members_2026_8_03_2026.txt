Staff Assistant - MEM-248-26
Representative Mike Levin (CA-49) seeks Staff Assistant for his DC office. This position is
responsible for managing the front desk, answering the DC office phones, operating the DC
office internship program, coordinating constituents’ tour and flag requests, planning and
drafting basic correspondence, and handling additional administrative duties of the office
as assigned.
The staff member in this role interacts regularly with constituents and should have a
friendly, helpful demeanor. The ideal candidate should have strong written and verbal
communications skills, extreme attention to detail, and a positive attitude. A working
knowledge of legislative process and major policy issues is a plus.
This staff member should also be a self-starter who likes to solve problems and works well
with a team. Strong organizational skills and a passion for helping others are essential to
this role.
Possession of a driver’s license, a clean driving record, and a personal vehicle are
preferred in order to drive the Member in DC. Driving responsibilities could include
frequent short distances during days that the House is in session and some driving in the
evening.

position is September 21, 2026.

Office Description:
The House Office of the Legislative Counsel provides legislative drafting services to the
committees and Members of the United States House of Representatives on a
nonpartisan, impartial, and confidential basis. The Office works with committees and
Members to understand their policies in order to draft clear, concise, and legally effective
legislative language to carry out those policies.
The Front Office serves as the initial point of contact for the Office by providing assistance
to clients, welcoming visitors to the Office, answering general questions, and referring
requests received by the Office to the appropriate attorney, drafting team, or paralegal.
Position Description:
The Front Office Supervisor supervises a team of three Front Office Assistants who provide
essential administrative and clerical support services to the staff within the House Office
of the Legislative Counsel (HOLC). The Front Office Supervisor oversees the operations of
the Front Office and communicates with Office management regarding the operation and
performance of the Front Office.
The Front Office Supervisor may work on special projects at the request and under the
direction of the Legislative Counsel. The Front Office Supervisor is a supervisory position
and works under the direction and supervision of the Legislative Counsel. After an initial
90-day period, the Front Office Supervisor is eligible to telework up to one day per week, at
the discretion of the Legislative Counsel.
Primary Duties and Responsibilities:
• Supervises a team of three Front Office Assistants.
• Ensures the effective day-to-day operations of the Front Office.
• Develops and maintains general working knowledge and understanding of HOLC’s
practices and the legislative process.
• Serves as a primary representative of HOLC by providing friendly, professional, and
helpful customer service to office visitors and callers, including by addressing
general and routine inquiries and forwarding messages as appropriate.
• Ensures that the Front Office has adequate in-office presence and phone coverage
during official business hours and after hours, as necessary, including by
coordinating the schedules of the Front Office Assistants.
• Carefully assesses and distributes client requests to attorneys and paralegals by
phone and email.
• Ensures the work of the Front Office team is performed accurately and consistently.
