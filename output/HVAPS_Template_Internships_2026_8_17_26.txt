DC Fall 2026 Internship - MEM-259-26
The Office of Congressman Greg Casar is Hiring for DC Fall 2026 Internship
The Office of Congressman Greg Casar (TX-35) is currently seeking a candidate for one
paid internship during the Fall 2026 term. This internship is full-time and in-person in our
Washington DC office. Applications are due by Friday, August 21, 12PM ET.
The Fall 2026 internship will run from September 1 to December 17. Interns are paid
$18.40/hour at the close of each month. Fall internships are 40 hours/week within the
hours of 9 am-6 pm, Monday through Friday.
DUTIES
This internship provides a valuable opportunity to contribute to the daily operations of a
congressional office and gain first-hand experience of working in a district office. Interns
are critical to the operations of a congressional office. Various intern responsibilities may
include but are not limited to: greeting guests, answering phones, logging constituent
phone calls, responding to emails, opening and sorting mail, drafting constituent letters,
performing research, assisting staff with events, meetings, communications tasks, and
various day-to-day office tasks.
QUALIFIED APPLICANT REQUIREMENTS
• Interns must be at least 18 years of age.
• Texas ties, district ties, and Spanish language fluency are preferred but not
required.
• Employees of the House of Representatives must be Legal Permanent Residents or
US Citizens.
Successful internship candidates should:
• Demonstrate an interest in politics, policy, and/or public service.
• Possess a professional demeanor and an upbeat disposition while interacting with
visitors, constituents, and staff members in a fast-paced, highly dynamic
environment.
• Possess effective skills for verbal and written communications.

Our office is committed to building a team with diverse lived experiences that equips us to

Video Internship - MEM-249-26
VIDEO INTERNSHIP
The House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform Democratic Staff seeks a
motivated and reliable Video Production Intern this fall. Interns will be responsible for
helping with clipping streams of sessions, editing and captioning videos, helping with
shoots, scripting content and other and administrative tasks as assigned. Successful
candidates will possess extreme familiarity with Adobe Premiere Pro. Additional
experiences with Adobe After Effects and Photoshop are a plus.
Ability to work in a fast-paced, rapid response environment is a must. Candidates will be
expected to work in-person in Washington, DC, with opportunities for remote work during
Congressional recesses. Full-time candidates with diverse backgrounds and experiences
are strongly encouraged to apply.
