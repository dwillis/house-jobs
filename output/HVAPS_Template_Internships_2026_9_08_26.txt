Law Clerk - MEM-256-26
U.S. House of Representatives, Committee on Homeland Security: Oversight,
Investigations, and Accountability Subcommittee (Majority)
Description: Law clerks will have the opportunity to work with the Committee’s policy,
oversight, and investigative teams, conducting legal and policy research, reviewing
documents, and preparing materials for hearings, markups, and other Committee
activities.
Length: Specific dates will be determined on a case-by-case basis, but the fall clerkship
period runs from September to December. Preference will be given to candidates who are
available at least three days per week.
Pay: This is a paid position, and the Committee is happy to work with applicants seeking
academic credit.
Requirements: Applicants must be current law students with strong research and writing
skills who are enthusiastic about the Committee’s mission to protect the homeland and
strengthen national security.
